Featured Projects

Online Retail Sales Dashboard

I built an interactive Streamlit dashboard that transforms approximately 1.07 million UK online-retail transactions into decision-ready sales insights. It brings revenue performance, customer and product trends, market performance, and seasonal patterns into one interface so a stakeholder can quickly see what is selling, where revenue is coming from, and how sales are changing over time.

What I built

A Python-based analytics application with data cleaning, KPI calculations, interactive filters, trend analysis, product rankings, country-level analysis, and automated business insights.

Technical stack

Python, Pandas, Plotly, Streamlit, Excel, Git and GitHub.

Key challenge

Converting a large, messy transaction dataset into reliable metrics while handling cancellations, invalid prices or quantities, missing descriptions, date filtering, and aggregation across markets and products.

Business outcome

The dashboard turns transaction-level data into a self-service view of sales performance, helping users identify revenue trends, leading products and markets, and areas that warrant further investigation.
